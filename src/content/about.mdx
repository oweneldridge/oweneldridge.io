I'm Owen. I work on the data-integrity side of pharmacy claims: platform
migrations, reconciliation, and the incidents where the numbers stop
agreeing. Claims are financial records in a regulated industry, so a wrong
number here is a compliance problem rather than a failed test.

<Note>

Writing software since 2021, in pharmacy claims since 2025. The [resume](/resume/) lists employers and dates.

</Note>

Before I wrote software for a living I spent years on the operations side
of consumer finance. First dealership finance and insurance, then managing
dealer performance across a territory, where I also built the reporting
that showed how the business was doing. Then director of operations at a
car-leasing startup, working between the dealerships and our engineers:
mapping dealer inventory into live vehicle feeds, and helping set up the
lease calculations for each tax jurisdiction and each lender, since every
financing arm calculates a lease its own way. That is where I learned what
the numbers meant. The engineering came after: point of sale and checkout,
then technical lead on payments at Indigo Tech Group, a Mindbody company (it has since been
renamed Kite Technologies), working on the parts of a payment platform
where compliance lives. Merchant onboarding,
card-present processing, sanctions and beneficial-ownership screening,
PCI.

<Note>

The payments years are written up in [The compliance edge of a payments platform](/projects/payments-compliance/).

</Note>

The habit I care most about is keeping what the data proves apart from
what it only suggests. I label an estimate as an estimate, I say when
something hasn't been measured, and I correct my own findings before
anyone acts on them. I've had to do that last one under real pressure.

I run my own infrastructure for related reasons. Photos, documents,
passwords, books, and an offline copy of Wikipedia live on hardware I
administer, behind a network I audit myself. Some of that is practical,
but mostly I think people should be able to own their tools and their
data, and I build that way.

<Note>

What that looks like: [Spectre](/projects/spectre/).

</Note>

I care about accessibility, because software that only works for some
people is broken. I care about durability too: the best tools I use are
decades old, and I'd like to build things that last that long.

<Note>

Accessibility work: [Spearmint](/projects/spearmint/). Durability as a design goal: [Basalt](/projects/basalt/).

</Note>

Outside of work I play classical guitar, take long bike rides, travel, and
cook for whoever shows up.
