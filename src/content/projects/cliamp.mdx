cliamp is a terminal music player written in Go, with more than four
thousand stars on GitHub. It isn't my project. Over a few days in
September I sent it five pull requests, all merged, that make podcast
episodes seekable, remember where you stopped in each one, and let you
browse the shows you subscribe to.

## Episodes that played like live radio

Podcast episodes played as if they were live streams. The header read
LIVE, seeking did nothing, and a seek sent over the player's IPC interface
reported success without moving. The cause was the choice of pipeline:
everything fetched over HTTP went down the streaming path. A response with
a finite length and none of the headers internet radio sends is a file,
not a broadcast, so it now goes to the buffered FFmpeg pipeline, which can
seek. The episode's length comes from the feed until the download
finishes, then from ffprobe, because feeds understate it. Without FFmpeg
installed, nothing changes.

<Note>

The fix: [#474](https://github.com/bjarneo/cliamp/pull/474).

</Note>

## Remembering where you were

The podcast provider kept no state, so every episode started from the top.
It now stores listening position and played state per episode in a local
file, and the track list marks played and part-heard episodes. Most of the
work was in the edge cases: an episode that loses its metadata mid-play, a
CDN that rewrites episode URLs, same-titled episodes with no GUID (told
apart by publication date), and a progress file written by a newer version
of cliamp. That last one used to be read as far as possible and rewritten
in the older format, quietly dropping whatever the newer version had
stored. Now it's left untouched and saving is skipped for the session.

<Note>

Position and played state: [#475](https://github.com/bjarneo/cliamp/pull/475). Browsing subscribed shows: [#476](https://github.com/bjarneo/cliamp/pull/476).

</Note>

The other two are smaller. Saved playlists can be appended to the one
that's playing, and the queue gets a view of its own. The visualizer
remembers its mode between launches and can hide the episode title, since
the full-screen view is the one most likely to be on a shared screen.

<Note>

Playlists and the queue: [#477](https://github.com/bjarneo/cliamp/pull/477). The visualizer: [#479](https://github.com/bjarneo/cliamp/pull/479).

</Note>
